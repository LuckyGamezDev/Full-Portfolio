#### Website Description:

This website will allow users to view information about our club, The Game Buddies, offer the option to join, and interact with the community. Our gaming club is here to have a nice community where people can talk about all of their thoughts and interests about upcoming, newly released, or already-existing games. The website also features written articles by our community and us, the club owners about all sorts of games. The physical club is where the community actually meets with each other in person, where they can either just hang out, or play games on-site. 



The website will have a homepage, articles page, and a page to join our community. It will also have an about us section, location of the physical club, and other relevant info.



#### Audience:

The Game Buddies is intended only for people who are actively interested in gaming and are willing to talk about all types of games. Of course, those who are rude to the community will not be able to join, or else, if already joined, be banned.



#### User stories:

1. as someone who is interested in joining the club, I would like to know what the community is like before I join it, because I want to make sure I fit in with the community.
2. as a member, I would like to have scheduled meetings every now and then to be able to connect more deeply with the rest of the community
3. as someone who is new to gaming (but interested), I would appreciate to have some sort of guide for beginners that explains the essentials that one should know, because I would otherwise have difficulty understanding what others are talking about.

* At least all listed pages exist
